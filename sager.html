SAGE-R · Structured AI-Guided Education for Research

From AI assistance to research ownership.

SAGE-R is a research-informed assurance framework that extends SAGE into higher-degree research. It guides candidates’ use of AI across the research lifecycle and gives supervisors Defend—a practical way to check whether candidates understand the work, verify its evidence independently, and can defend their claims.

Designed to fit existing candidature milestones and supervisory conversations.

A rule for every AI-assisted research task

Use AI. Keep the research yours.

Apply these six actions whenever AI assists your research.

  1. 01

    Check permission

    Is this use permitted by your institution, ethics approval, funder or publisher?

  2. 02

    Protect the research

    Keep confidential data and unpublished work out of unapproved tools.

  3. 03

    Set the boundary

    Decide what AI may help with and what judgement stays yours.

  4. 04

    Verify independently

    Check against original sources, data, code, methods or other evidence.

  5. 05

    Record and disclose

    Follow the record-keeping and disclosure requirements that apply to your research.

  6. 06

    Defend the work

    Explain, justify, verify and stand behind your decisions and claims.

  7. You own the researchDecisions · Evidence · Claims

At the centre of the cycle: you own the research, including its decisions, evidence and claims.

If you cannot explain, justify, verify and stand behind it, it is not ready to enter the research.

The Defend conversation

A practical expression of scholarly responsibility.

“Why did you choose this method—and how did you check what the AI produced?”

An illustrative question in a supervisory meeting

01Explain

What did you do, and how did AI contribute?

02Justify

Why was this approach appropriate?

03Verify

What evidence beyond AI checks the result?

04Stand behind

Can you take responsibility for the claim?

Defend is a proportionate conversation about consequential research decisions, embedded in proposal, draft and analysis discussions. Required disclosure of tools and their use continues to apply.
