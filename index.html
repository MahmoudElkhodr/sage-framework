01 / The program

From permission
to purposeful practice.

SAGE brings the decisions about AI use, the learning process, and the evidence of capability into a connected approach. Each part has a distinct role—and practical resources to help you put it to work.

01
Develop the pedagogy

Teach students to exercise judgement.

The six-step SAGE framework makes evaluation, refinement and reflection part of learning with AI. Students work from their own understanding, test AI against disciplinary sources and justify their decisions.

02
Connect policy to practice

Make expectations actionable.

SAGE-AP translates permitted AI use into clear expectations for authorship, verification, disclosure and defensible work. Institutional and assessment rules set the permission boundary.

03
Build AI literacy

Prepare students for responsible use.

GenAI-101 offers eleven lessons on learning with AI, critical coaching, source checking and working with AI inside apps. The Student Guide turns the SAGE process into practical steps for assessment.

Explore learning resources ↗
04
Design assessment assurance

Make individual capability visible.

Defend asks students to explain, justify, apply or adapt their work. Design proportionate checks at selected points in learning, matched to the capability, discipline and cohort.

Design a Defend checkpoint ↗
The six-step pedagogy at the heart of SAGEExplore the learning process
  1. 01 · GenerateBegin with your own thinking, then use AI to create material to examine.
  2. 02 · EvaluateCheck the output against authoritative sources and task requirements.
  3. 03 · RefineAccept, modify or reject suggestions with evidence and reasons.
  4. 04 · AI CriticInvite AI to challenge the work. Judge the critique yourself.
  5. 05 · ReflectExplain what changed, where AI failed and what you learned.
  6. 06 · DefendDemonstrate that you can explain, apply and adapt the capability.

Defend is a design principle, with assurance distributed where learning outcomes require direct confirmation. Its format and timing depend on the assessment context.

02 / Research informs the program

Built through inquiry.
Refined in classrooms.

SAGE has developed through successive studies of how students use AI, exercise judgement and demonstrate understanding. Classroom findings inform the pedagogy, policy and resources.

01 / DESIGNStart with a learning problem

Design activities around the capability students need to develop.

02 / STUDYGather classroom evidence

Examine student work, reflections and implementation experiences.

03 / EVALUATETest the assumptions

Investigate what the evidence reveals—and what it cannot establish.

04 / REFINEBring findings into practice

Improve the program and make the next implementation more informed.

A traceable research foundation.

The program draws on constructive alignment, evaluative judgement, self-regulated learning and situated practice. Its publication record distinguishes direct SAGE studies from the wider scholarship on AI in education.
