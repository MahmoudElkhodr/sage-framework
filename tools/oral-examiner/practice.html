Getting Sage ready

The first time, your browser downloads the examiner character and a speech recognition model (the voice and speech models can total several hundred megabytes on a first visit). Later visits are much faster.

Microphone check

Press the button and say a sentence such as "My assignment is about network design". The test stops by itself when you pause, just as Sage does during the session, and it also measures the background noise in your room.

This is practice, not an assessment. Nothing you do here counts towards your grade. Using ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI to answer for you would only rob you of the practice: your real oral examination will be with a person, and only your own understanding will help you there.

  • Sit somewhere quiet and speak clearly, as you would to a lecturer.
  • Sage listens automatically as soon as she finishes each question, so answer straight away, as you would in a real oral examination. Each answer can be up to 1 minute.
  • Sage replies when you have finished. If you pause to think, "Still listening" is shown and you may carry on; you can also press "I'm done" (or the Enter key) as soon as you have finished.
  • How quickly you begin each answer is recorded for your teacher.
  • Stay on this page during the session. Leaving the page is noted for your teacher.
