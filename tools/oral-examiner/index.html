Practise defending your work, out loud

Defend Practice puts the Defend step of the SAGE Framework into students' hands. Sage, an AI examiner, reads the assignment brief and the student's own submission, then holds a short spoken conversation about it. Students receive an indicative mark and specific feedback straight away, and may practise again.

How it works

The teacher sets the base questions that every student must be asked. Sage rephrases each one around what the individual student actually wrote, and adds adaptive follow-up questions when an answer is vague or does not match the submission. When the time is up, an AI marker reviews the conversation against the rubric and returns a mark, evidence-based comments and concrete improvements.

Speech is recognised by an open-source Whisper model running inside the student's browser, so recordings never leave the device; only the transcribed text is sent to the examiner. The examiner's voice is also generated in the browser where the device supports it.

This is a practice and formative tool. Marks are AI-generated, indicative only and may differ from a human marker's judgement. Teachers can review every attempt, including transcripts and an authenticity indicator.
